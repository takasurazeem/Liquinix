Icons

tahoe\   MacTahoe icon theme by vinceliuice (https://github.com/vinceliuice/MacTahoe-icon-theme), GPL-3.0,
         converted from SVG to PNG by tools\icones-mactahoe.ps1. Credits in tahoe\CREDITOS.txt.

The other icon folders the code looks for (sequoia\, simbolos\, extra\, tahoe-macosicons\, bloqueio\) held
Apple or third-party artwork that cannot be redistributed, and were removed. When a folder is missing, the
Shell and Finder fall back to the Windows icons and their own drawings.
